# External replication protocol lock — negative-set evaluation validity

**Study:** How much apparent DDI link-prediction performance is explained by negative-set structure?

**Status at lock:** The GoldD2/ATC5 pilot has already been observed and is therefore not a prospective replication. The external outcomes below have not been inspected at the time this protocol is committed. This is a timestamped public protocol lock in version control, **not an OSF preregistration**.

## Conceptual hypothesis

A negative edge in a drug–drug interaction benchmark is not a neutral complement of the positive graph. If negative examples have a different endpoint-degree/popularity distribution, graph models can achieve high AUC by exploiting benchmark structure rather than pair-specific interaction information. Matching negative examples to positive endpoint degree should reduce this shortcut and may change model rankings.

## External benchmarks fixed before outcome inspection

1. **BioSNAP ChCh-Miner**: positive DrugBank DDI graph. Source file `ChCh-Miner_durgbank-chem-chem.tsv` from the public SkipGNN mirror of BioSNAP. No curated negatives are supplied; P1/P2/P3/P5 are generated by the locked protocol.
2. **CRESCENDDI**: public positive and negative drug–drug–event controls. For this study the event dimension is collapsed *before modeling* to unordered drug pairs. A positive pair is any pair appearing in any positive DDE record. A curated negative pair is a pair appearing in a negative DDE record **and never in the positive pair set**. This pair-level aggregation is fixed before model evaluation.

Raw third-party benchmark files are downloaded during CI and are not redistributed in this repository.

## Train/test design

For each benchmark and seed, 20% of positive edges are held out. The training adjacency contains only the remaining positive edges. **No model is fitted to held-out positive/negative labels.** Every model score is a deterministic function of the training graph.

Eight locked graph-only scores: endpoint popularity, preferential attachment, common neighbors, Adamic–Adar, resource allocation, Jaccard, spectral-SVD cosine, and diffusion-SVD cosine.

## Negative protocols

- **P1 uniform random non-edges** — conventional baseline.
- **P2 degree-proportional non-edges** — samples endpoints in proportion to training degree.
- **P3 degree-matched non-edges** — primary corrective; matches the unordered endpoint training-degree-bin signature of each held-out positive without using model scores or outcomes.
- **P5 configuration rewiring** — independent corrective preserving the held-out endpoint multiset through rejected double-edge rewiring.
- **P4 curated negatives** — CRESCENDDI only; pair-level curated negative controls after exclusion of every pair present in the positive DDE set.

Any protocol yielding fewer than 100 negative examples in a seed is marked dropped and is not silently replaced.

## Locked endpoints

### H1 — primary: structural inflation
For each of the eight models and each external benchmark: `inflation = AUC(P1 uniform) - AUC(P3 degree-matched)`.

Inference: paired seed-level sign-flip test (20,000 permutations), 5,000 bootstrap CI for the mean, Holm correction across the eight models **within benchmark**.

Primary support criterion: at least 6/8 models have positive inflation with Holm-adjusted p<0.05 in the same external benchmark. Results remain reported if the criterion fails.

### H2 — descriptive: null-model share
For each protocol, endpoint-popularity performance relative to the best non-null graph score: `(AUC_popularity-0.5)/(AUC_best_non-null-0.5)` when the denominator is positive. No confirmatory p-value is attached to this ratio.

### H3 — secondary: ranking instability
Model-pair ordering under P1 is compared with P3 and P5. Pairwise order reversals require opposite mean deltas and Holm-adjusted p<0.05 for both protocol-specific paired deltas. Kendall tau across the eight model rankings is reported descriptively.

## Outcome-neutral diagnostics fixed before outcome inspection

- node and positive-edge counts;
- positive and negative sample sizes for every seed/protocol;
- P3 and P5 availability/drop counts;
- CRESCENDDI raw DDE pair aggregation counts and positive-overlap exclusions;
- SHA-256 of every downloaded external source file.

## Interpretation boundaries

This study evaluates **benchmark/evaluation validity**, not clinical DDI prediction accuracy and not the clinical safety of Anti-DDI pairs. A large P1→P3 decline supports a structural-shortcut interpretation. A weak or absent decline is equally reportable. CRESCENDDI is particularly informative as a contrast because its negatives were curated rather than sampled as arbitrary missing graph edges.

The previously withdrawn text-classifier/RIDI experiment is not part of this protocol or evidence chain.
