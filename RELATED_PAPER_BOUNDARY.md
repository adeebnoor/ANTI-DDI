# Related-paper and duplicate-publication boundary for the Science project

## Purpose

This document defines the publication boundary between the broad Science-targeted biomedical-AI benchmark study on `science-structural-shortcuts` and the Anti-DDI resource/manuscript line. It is a disclosure and governance document, not a claim that any particular related manuscript is currently under editorial consideration.

## Distinct scientific questions

### Anti-DDI resource/manuscript line

Primary scientific object: candidate non-interacting drug–drug pairs and their evidence state.

Core contribution: construct, audit and transparently qualify a tiered candidate reference resource using co-exposure, observed adverse-event evidence, label screening and a frozen DDI positive reference. The structural-degree analysis is a benchmark diagnostic used to qualify the resource and constrain interpretation.

Claims that belong to this line include resource composition, evidence tiers, DDI-specific limitations, candidate/reference provenance and the explicit prohibition on interpreting candidate absence of evidence as clinical safety.

### Science-targeted study

Primary scientific object: **the scientific decision induced by biomedical relation-prediction benchmark construction**.

Core contribution: test an integrated decision chain across multiple independent relation families:

1. structural opportunity can create benchmark discrimination without pair-specific biology;
2. learned model families respond unequally;
3. the apparent winning model can reverse;
4. the selected model can redirect the identity of top-ranked biological hypotheses beyond training instability;
5. those choices can concentrate different later or independent evidence at the discovery frontier.

The Science study uses DTI, PPI, compound–disease and disease–gene systems, BioGRID historical/future releases, independent ChEMBL evidence, stability-controlled hypothesis-identity analyses and a contemporary-model challenge. These experiments are not the Anti-DDI resource paper.

## Permitted overlap

The following overlap is scientifically legitimate if disclosed:

- Anti-DDI as the motivating evidence-state example;
- the frozen GoldD2/ATC5 positive reference as provenance for the DDI-specific boundary analysis;
- previously released Anti-DDI code/data assets when cited as prior or companion resources;
- general methodological language needed to describe the provenance of reused public/frozen assets.

Reused datasets or analyses must never be presented as newly generated by the Science study if they were already reported elsewhere.

## Materially new Science evidence

The following evidence is independent of the Anti-DDI resource contribution and is central to the Science manuscript:

- four-relation-family structural-null atlas;
- learned-model sensitivity across DTI, CtD and DaG;
- benchmark-induced model-rank reversals;
- stability-controlled DTI hypothesis turnover;
- independent PPI hypothesis-identity replication on the 70,041,100-pair historical candidate universe;
- BioGRID 5.0.250 → 5.0.261 later-evidence experiment;
- paired-bootstrap uncertainty for future-evidence recovery;
- prespecified independent ChEMBL 37 DTI evidence analysis;
- frozen contemporary-model challenge (GraphBAN);
- any final cross-domain reporting standard supported by these new results.

## Submission rule

The **same manuscript cannot be submitted to two journals simultaneously**. If an Anti-DDI manuscript is under consideration when the Science manuscript is submitted, the Science cover letter and submission disclosures must identify it as a related manuscript and explain the scientific distinction. If the journal requests the related manuscript, provide it.

The Science manuscript must not reproduce substantial text, figures or tables from a related manuscript without explicit attribution and editorially appropriate handling. New figures should be built around the cross-domain decision chain, not reused Anti-DDI resource figures.

## Cover-letter disclosure language — working version

> This manuscript is scientifically distinct from our related Anti-DDI resource work. The related work develops and qualifies a drug–drug interaction candidate reference resource and uses structural analysis as a resource-diagnostic limitation. The present study addresses a broader and different question: whether benchmark construction propagates through model selection into the identity of biological hypotheses prioritized for discovery and into later or independent evidence recovery. The present manuscript adds independent drug–target, protein–protein, compound–disease and disease–gene analyses; stability-controlled hypothesis-ranking experiments; historical BioGRID future-evidence validation; independent ChEMBL validation; and a contemporary-model challenge. Any reused frozen Anti-DDI assets are identified as prior/companion resources rather than presented as new results.

## Final pre-submission check

Before submission, compare the final Science manuscript against every active or published Anti-DDI manuscript and mark each figure, table, dataset and paragraph as one of: `new`, `reused-with-citation`, `related-but-not-reproduced`, or `remove`. The cover letter should then describe the overlap in one concise paragraph without minimizing it or overstating independence.
